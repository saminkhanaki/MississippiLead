About Mississippi Lead

Independent local news for the people of Mississippi

Mississippi Lead is an independent news publication covering Jackson, the Gulf Coast, and communities across the Magnolia State. We report on local business, public affairs, and the issues that directly affect Mississippi residents — without a national editorial agenda.

We started Mississippi Lead because local news matters. Not national takes on Mississippi, not wire stories rewritten for a local byline — but original, ground-level reporting on the people, institutions, and decisions that shape daily life in this state.

Our coverage spans three areas: news and investigative reporting on public affairs and local incidents; business coverage focused on Mississippi's economy, job market, and local enterprises; and lifestyle coverage of the restaurants, services, and community institutions that define the Gulf Coast and Jackson metro.

How we work

Every story we publish starts with a question worth answering — not a press release, not a trending topic on national media. We research independently, verify before publishing, and when we don't have enough information to give a complete picture, we say so.

We cover Mississippi stories that larger outlets overlook or cover only at the surface. When a verdict raises more questions than it answers, we dig. When a policy change affects tens of thousands of residents, we explain what it actually means in plain language. When a local business or institution deserves honest scrutiny, we apply it.

Local focus

Every story connects to Mississippi — Jackson, the Gulf Coast, or communities across the state. We don't cover national news unless it has a direct, specific impact here.

Original reporting

We research and write our own stories. We don't republish wire content or repackage what other outlets have already covered.

Plain language

We write for Mississippi residents, not policy insiders. If a topic is complicated, we make it understandable — without oversimplifying what matters.

Center-right perspective

We cover Mississippi from a perspective that respects individual responsibility, limited government, and the practical concerns of working families in this state.

Coverage areas

Our news section covers local incidents, public affairs, political decisions, and investigative reports — stories with real consequences for Mississippi residents. Our business section focuses on the local economy: hiring trends, business openings and closings, economic development, and practical guides for navigating services in the Jackson area and Gulf Coast. Our lifestyle section covers the restaurants, healthcare providers, and community institutions that define life in Mississippi — reviewed honestly and reported independently.
