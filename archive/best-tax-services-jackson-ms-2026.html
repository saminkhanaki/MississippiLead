The 7 Best Tax Preparation Services in Jackson, Mississippi

#1 Jackson Hewitt Tax Service — Best for Accessibility and Same-Day Refund Advances

As the second-largest tax preparation company in the United States, Jackson Hewitt processes approximately two million returns each year across more than 5,200 locations — including 2,600 inside Walmart stores. For Jackson, MS residents, that footprint translates into genuinely easy access, with locations spread throughout the metro area. The company has built its brand around low-cost solutions backed by year-round support, making it a reliable choice for filers who want professional help without a steep price tag.

What distinguishes Jackson Hewitt from other national chains is its emphasis on one-on-one service: each client is paired with a specialist who engages with their individual situation rather than moving through a generic checklist. For residents who need fast access to funds, the Tax Refund Advance (TRA) loan program offers same-day approvals of up to $3,500 — a meaningful option during financially tight periods.

  • Maximum Refund Guarantee: If a competing preparer calculates a larger refund, Jackson Hewitt refunds your preparation fee and pays an additional $100.
  • Lifetime Accuracy Guarantee: IRS penalties and interest resulting from software errors are covered by the company.
  • Same-day refund advance loans up to $3,500 with rapid approval — no waiting for the IRS.
  • Dedicated audit support if your return is selected for IRS review.
⚠ Limitation: Jackson Hewitt does not offer a fully free online filing option, and its digital platform does not support all niche tax forms — a drawback for filers with complex or non-standard situations.

#2 H&R Block — Best Overall for Flexibility, Transparency, and Free Filing

With 70 years in operation, H&R Block is the most established name in professional tax preparation. The company serves more than 20 million clients annually across 12,000 offices worldwide and maintains a physical location within five miles of most Americans — making it one of the most accessible options in Jackson, MS. Its internal Tax Institute, staffed by tax attorneys and Certified Public Accountants, continuously monitors legislative changes to ensure clients never miss a credit or deduction they are entitled to claim.

H&R Block's defining strength is flexibility. Jackson residents can choose to file in person at a local office, upload documents through the secure MyBlock portal for virtual assistance, or simply drop off paperwork and let a professional handle the rest. For clients with simple returns, a genuinely free federal and state filing option is available with no income restrictions — a rare feature among full-service providers.

  • No Surprise Guarantee: Exact pricing is confirmed before any work begins.
  • AI Tax Assist: 24/7 automated tax guidance for answering complex questions at any time.
  • Three filing options: in-person, fully virtual through MyBlock, or document drop-off.
  • Free federal and state filing available for simple returns, with no income cap.
⚠ Limitation: Pricing increases quickly for investment income or self-employment scenarios, and access to direct professional assistance on some platforms requires an additional fee.

#3 TurboTax (North Jackson) — Best for Self-Employed Filers and Full-Service Handoff

The North Jackson location of TurboTax combines the power of Intuit's industry-leading software platform with locally available, face-to-face expertise. The office focuses on maximizing refunds through thorough identification of every applicable deduction, and clients can choose between in-office appointments or fully remote filing with secure document management.

For freelancers, gig workers, and small business owners, TurboTax's dedicated self-employment tools are among the most comprehensive available in the Jackson area, with expense tracking features built specifically for independent contractors. The Full Service option allows clients to hand off the entire filing process — from document collection to final IRS submission — to a verified tax expert.

  • 100% Accuracy Guarantee: All IRS penalties and interest caused by calculation errors are reimbursed in full.
  • 7-Year Support Window: Returns are backed for accuracy review and audit assistance for seven years after filing.
  • Full-service handoff option — complete delegation to a certified tax professional.
  • Best specialized tools in Jackson for self-employed individuals and small business owners.
⚠ Limitation: The North Jackson office may not handle highly complex corporate filings in person. Fees for cryptocurrency investors and real estate filers are higher than comparable local alternatives.

Trinity's client retention rate reflects the quality of its service. Many filers have worked exclusively with this firm for over a decade, returning each year because their preparer knows their financial history, understands their goals, and brings genuine care to every return. An online client portal enables easy document access and digital payments.

3 Tax Services in Jackson, MS You Should Avoid

Not every tax preparer in the Jackson area operates with integrity. The following three providers have documented histories of fraud, legal violations, or serious operational failures. Regardless of pricing or convenience, filers should avoid all three.

⛔ American Tax Service — Fraud Allegations and Fabricated Reviews

American Tax Service has accumulated a record that goes far beyond poor service. The firm has been documented charging fees in excess of $1,000 while clients reported that government assistance payments were redirected into management accounts rather than deposited with the taxpayer. Former employees have faced tax fraud convictions, and management has been credibly accused of purchasing fabricated positive reviews to inflate its online reputation. The firm holds no accreditation from the Better Business Bureau.

⛔ Fas Tax — Federal Legal Action and Systematic Return Fraud

Fas Tax is the subject of formal federal proceedings, including a petition for permanent injunction based on evidence of systematic fraudulent return preparation and improper application of tax credits. Client complaints on record describe consistent operational failures: incorrect bank account numbers entered on filed returns, unsigned official forms submitted to the IRS, and executive staff who become unreachable during peak filing season — precisely when clients need support most.

⛔ Sunbelt Tax Service — Federal Convictions for $3.5 Million Fraud Scheme

Sunbelt Tax Service represents the most serious case in this guide. Its principals were convicted on federal charges connected to a $3.5 million fraud scheme involving the fabrication of more than 4,500 tax returns. Clients additionally reported aggressive behavior from staff following contract signing, along with undisclosed fees that dramatically increased costs after the fact. This provider should be avoided without exception.

How to Choose the Right Tax Service in Jackson, MS

The right tax preparer depends on your specific situation. If accessibility and fast refund advances matter most, Jackson Hewitt and H&R Block offer the broadest reach across the Jackson metro area. If you are self-employed or operating a small business, TurboTax's North Jackson office and C&C Financial Services offer the strongest specialized tools available locally. For clients who value continuity and personalized attention, Trinity Financial, B & B Tax Services, and Brooks Consulting all represent excellent community-rooted options.

Before You Sign Anything — Three Rules

1. Verify credentials. Ask for the preparer's PTIN (IRS Preparer Tax Identification Number) before handing over any documents.

2. Get a written fee estimate. Legitimate preparers will confirm pricing before starting your return. Walk away from anyone who refuses.

3. Check for errors and omissions insurance. This protects you if a mistake on your return results in IRS penalties.
