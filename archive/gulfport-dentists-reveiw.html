Best Dentists in Gulfport, MS: Top 10 Dental Practices Reviewed and Ranked for 2026

March 12, 2026 • Gulfport, MS • By Local Desk

A comprehensive, independently reported review of Gulfport, Mississippi's leading dental practices — covering clinical quality, patient experience, pricing transparency, and the three offices residents should approach with caution.

Finding the right dentist in Gulfport, Mississippi is not as simple as picking the office closest to home. The Gulf Coast's dental market spans nationally affiliated chains, long-established family practices, specialty clinics, and pediatric-only offices — each with distinct strengths, pricing structures, and patient experiences. Some practices have built decades of loyalty through gentle, personalized care. Others invest heavily in cutting-edge technology. A few, unfortunately, have generated consistent complaints that any informed patient deserves to know about before booking an appointment.

This review evaluated fourteen dental practices active in the greater Gulfport area. Assessments draw on documented service offerings, practitioner credentials, patient-reported experiences across multiple seasons, pricing transparency, insurance handling, and the depth and consistency of feedback from the local community. Ten practices emerged as genuinely recommendable — each with a clear profile of who they serve best. The rankings below reflect that full picture, including the honest limitations alongside the strengths.

Editorial note: Our scoring considers clinical quality, patient experience, value and pricing transparency, and accessibility — including insurance acceptance, hours, and ease of booking.

#1 Beach View Family Dental

📍 9072 Lorraine Rd, Gulfport, MS 39507

Best for: Patients who value clinical excellence, spa-level comfort, and a dentist with academic-grade credentials.

Beach View Family Dental occupies a category of its own among Gulfport dental practices. Led by Dr. Karen Cruz — a former university faculty member and specialist in oral cancer prevention and tobacco cessation — the practice combines academic rigor with an environment patients consistently describe as more akin to a spa than a clinic. Every treatment room is equipped with a television, massage chair, blankets, and pillows. Appointments run on time. The team's clinical depth is reflected not just in credentials but in outcomes: Beach View is among the few Gulfport practices offering Reveal Clear Aligners and comprehensive sleep apnea treatment alongside standard family dentistry.

Financing is handled through Sunbit, with payment plans extending up to 72 months — one of the most flexible arrangements available in the market. CareCredit is also accepted.

STRENGTHS

  • ✔ Academic-grade clinical team.
  • ✔ Spa-quality patient environment.
  • ✔ Comprehensive specialty services.
  • ✔ Flexible long-term financing.

LIMITATIONS

  • ✘ Insurance billing inconsistencies.
  • ✘ Handling of disputes.

Bottom line: Beach View Family Dental is the highest-ceiling practice in Gulfport. For patients who want clinical excellence, modern technology, and a genuinely comfortable experience, it is the clear first choice — provided you verify insurance details upfront.

#2 Wheeler Family Dentistry

Best for: Patients with dental anxiety, families seeking a multigenerational practice, and anyone who values long-term continuity of care.

Wheeler Family Dentistry has served Gulfport-area residents for more than 35 years, making it one of the most tenured independent practices on the Gulf Coast. The father-and-son team of Dr. Edward and Dr. Tyler Wheeler has cultivated a patient base where loyalty spans decades — a rarity in any service industry. Dr. Tyler Wheeler in particular has earned a notable reputation for his calm, unhurried approach with anxious patients. Treatment rooms are fully private, which meaningfully reduces the anxiety of patients who feel exposed in open-bay configurations.

The practice accepts children as young as one year old and maintains a pediatric dental specialty alongside adult implant services. Treatment coordinator Lisa Morris (16 years with the practice) and hygienist Katie Chatman (11 years) contribute to the kind of staff continuity that makes patients feel genuinely known rather than processed.

STRENGTHS

  • ✔ 35+ years of community trust.
  • ✔ Exceptional anxiety management.
  • ✔ Full private treatment rooms.
  • ✔ Pediatric care from age 1.

LIMITATIONS

  • ✘ Long wait times for appointments.
  • ✘ Scheduling inconsistencies.

Bottom line: Wheeler Family Dentistry is Gulfport's most trusted long-term family practice. If you can wait for an appointment, it is worth it — especially for anxious patients and families with young children.

#3 Hillcrest Dental Group

Best for: Patients seeking a full-service group practice with multiple specialists, aesthetic services, and a social atmosphere.

Hillcrest Dental Group operates as Gulfport's most comprehensive multi-provider dental office, staffed by six dentists including Dr. Teresa Jones and Dr. Scarlett Johnson. The breadth of the team means that most patients never need an outside referral — orthodontics, oral surgery, cosmetic work, and general dentistry are all handled internally. The office has also cultivated a deliberately welcoming atmosphere: a coffee and tea bar, flavored water, and — uniquely — Botox services alongside dental treatment make Hillcrest feel less like a medical appointment and more like a lifestyle destination.

STRENGTHS

  • ✔ Six-dentist team. No referrals needed.
  • ✔ Lifestyle-oriented environment.
  • ✔ Strong administrative team.
  • ✔ Advanced imaging technology.

LIMITATIONS

  • ✘ Inconsistency across providers.

Bottom line: Hillcrest Dental Group is the right choice for patients who want everything in one place — and who appreciate an environment that treats a dental visit as something other than an ordeal.

#4 Zimmerman Family Dentistry

Best for: Patients who want a warm, low-anxiety experience combined with modern diagnostic technology.

Digital radiography cuts radiation exposure by 50 to 70 percent compared to traditional X-ray methods. Hygienist Sonya has become something of a local institution, regularly cited by name in patient feedback.

LIMITATIONS

How to Choose the Right Gulfport Dentist for Your Situation

The best dental practice in Gulfport depends entirely on what you need. For clinical excellence and spa-quality comfort, Beach View Family Dental is the clear leader. For long-term family care and the gentlest possible experience — especially for anxious adults or young children — Wheeler Family Dentistry's 35-year track record speaks for itself. Hillcrest Dental Group is the right choice for patients who want multiple specialists under one roof without the hassle of referrals.

For specialized complex care — TMJ disorders, All-on-4 implants, or minimally invasive restorations — Dr. Walter Guillot stands apart. For pediatric care, particularly for Medicaid-enrolled families, South Mississippi Smiles has no serious local competitor. And for patients whose primary barrier to dental care is financial, Healthy Smiles and Divine Dentistry both offer meaningful access programs that most Gulfport practices do not match.

Whatever your situation, one rule applies universally: request a written, itemized fee quote before authorizing any treatment, verify insurance coverage directly with your plan before your appointment, and do not hesitate to ask your provider to walk you through the treatment plan in plain language before work begins.
